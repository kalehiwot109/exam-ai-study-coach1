Design the Practice experience for StudyStreak, an AI-powered study platform for Ethiopian Grade 12 students preparing for the Ethiopian University Entrance Examination.

This is the core learning experience of the product.

Students should spend most of their time on this page.

The design should feel comparable in quality to Duolingo, Linear, Notion, Stripe Dashboard, and modern educational SaaS products.

Overall personality:

• Calm
• Focused
• Intelligent
• Encouraging
• Professional
• Minimal
• Distraction-free

Maintain the existing StudyStreak design language established in Mission Control.

Use the same color palette, typography, spacing, card styles, buttons, and modern SaaS appearance.

Do not introduce unnecessary decorations.

PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

Every screen should answer exactly one question.

Mission Overview

What am I about to study?

Question Screen

Can I answer this question?

Incorrect Answer

Why was I wrong?

Mission Complete

How did I perform?

Study Report

What should I improve?

Reflection

What should I do tomorrow?

Avoid mixing multiple purposes into one screen.

────────────────────────────

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Use progressive disclosure.

Do not overwhelm students with information.

Show only what is needed at each stage.

Allow students to navigate deeper only when necessary.

Keep layouts clean.

Use generous whitespace.

Maintain accessibility.

Maintain visual consistency with Mission Control.

The experience should feel like a premium educational product rather than a traditional online exam system.

The final result should feel polished enough to be presented as a production-ready startup product.
